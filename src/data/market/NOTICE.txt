Public market-data attribution and reuse

Numerical observations are from World Bank World Development Indicators (WDI).
Attribute each observation to World Bank WDI, its indicator code, observation
year, exact units, underlying source organization and actual retrieval date.
Source identities, query URLs and retrieval/revision dates are preserved in the
bundled records and indicator metadata. Do not replace those dates with the
bundle creation date.

World Bank summary terms: https://data.worldbank.org/summary-terms-of-use
Dataset-specific and third-party restrictions may apply. The repository's
software license does not grant rights to upstream data. Consult each source's
terms before redistributing it; a publicly accessible URL is not a reuse license.
Licensing for a discovery-directory entry remains unknown unless its retrieved
metadata establishes otherwise.

Retrieval expiry is an application freshness rule, not the observation year or
a determination that an annual statistic has become false. Display the actual
observation year and units. Macroeconomic observations are context, not proof of
customer demand, category revenue or a startup's addressable market.

Validore includes authenticated founder workspaces, private founder interviews, saved reports, Full/Lite durable AI workflows, source provenance, bounded shared research memory, account/credit synchronization, pricing/canvas/crowdfunding/pitch tools, and PDF/Word/PowerPoint/static-portfolio exports. The Lite final output ceiling is 5,000 tokens. Existing plan prices/action costs remain unchanged; real-provider quality and latency still require hosted acceptance.

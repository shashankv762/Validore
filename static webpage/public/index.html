Can a report tell me my idea will succeed?

No. Validore helps you organise research, examine assumptions, and plan what to test. AI scores and projections are estimates. Customer conversations and real-world experiments are still essential.

Where does the research come from?

When live search is available, reports use retrieved public sources and identify the evidence. When it isn’t, claims are marked as unverified estimates. The sample on this page is fictional; crowdfunding benchmarks in the app are illustrative.

Can I come back to my work?

Yes. Your account holds your saved ideas, reports, and Lean Canvas versions. Sign back in to revisit the findings and continue from your workspace. Read our privacy policy for how data is handled.

Why might the app take a moment to open?

This prototype rests when it isn’t being used. It can take about a minute to start again. When you arrive here, we send a small request to prepare the app while you explore. You can open it at any time; startup time can vary.

Does this page use tracking or advertising?

This landing page has no analytics, ads, or tracking cookies. It contacts the Validore app to check whether it is responding. The app has its own privacy and cookie information.
